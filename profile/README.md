
# Stardew Valley Expanded – A Massive Expansion Mod with New Characters, Locations, Quests, and Events

Stardew Valley Expanded is a popular fan-made expansion mod for Stardew Valley, adding new NPCs, locations, quests, storylines, and gameplay content. It features 28 characters, 58 locations, 278 events, 43 fish, three optional farm maps, new crops, festivals, and expanded exploration, enriching the original farming RPG experience.

## Expanded World and New Locations

Stardew Valley Expanded introduces 58 additional locations that extend the original game's explorable world. These areas include new environments, hidden places, and locations connected to character storylines and progression. Some areas become accessible through quests and gameplay achievements, encouraging players to explore beyond the familiar boundaries of Pelican Town.

The expansion also reimagines existing vanilla locations and introduces an updated world map that reflects the additional areas. Location messages provide further environmental storytelling, giving players additional details to discover throughout the world.

## New Characters and Storylines

The mod adds 28 new NPCs and 278 character events, significantly expanding the social and narrative aspects of Stardew Valley. New characters have their own personalities, dialogue, relationships, and individual stories that become available through gameplay progression.

Character events add new narrative interactions and provide additional context for the expanded world. These additions give players more opportunities to develop friendships, discover character backgrounds, and experience stories beyond the original game's content.

## New Farm Maps and Gameplay Content

Stardew Valley Expanded provides three optional farm layouts designed to offer different farming experiences:

- **Frontier Farm** – An expansive farm bordering the Ferngill Republic Frontier, featuring wild trees and hidden secrets.
- **Grandpa's Farm** – A custom farm layout designed specifically for the expansion, with shortcuts and distinctive landmarks.
- **Immersive Farm 2 Remastered** – A large farm optimized for multiplayer and players who want additional building and farming space.

Additional content includes new fish, crops, objects, festivals, quests, and gameplay activities. Grampleton Fields is also available as an optional large farmable and buildable area east of Shearwater Bridge.

## Compatibility and Configuration

Stardew Valley Expanded supports Stardew Valley 1.6 and provides configuration options that allow players to customize selected aspects of the experience. These options include seasonal environmental changes and visual adjustments.

The expansion also offers compatibility with other mods, although individual compatibility depends on the installed modifications and their requirements. Players should consult the official documentation and mod page before combining large content expansions.
